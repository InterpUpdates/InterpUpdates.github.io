Sparsity-vs-EM frontier (λ sweep)

Same sparse-mask method at different L0 penalties λ. EM is a THRESHOLD: it needs ~20% of neurons to move. Moderate sparsity (λ=3e-5, 19.2%) gives the strongest EM (beats dense, matches LoRA); the aggressive default λ=3e-4 (~2%) shows none even at 10 epochs. Mask stays ~200-2000x sparser than LoRA (168M params) throughout. base: EM 0%, mean-align 91. LoRA r32: 1.3%, 83.

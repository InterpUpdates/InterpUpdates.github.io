Sparse-mask EM reproduction — mask vs LoRA vs the paper's checkpoint, and the full sweep

234 configs on Qwen3.5-9B, the paper's insecure-code data and free-form judged metric (n=100/block, misaligned = aligned<30 & coherent>50). Base control 1/2291 = 0.04%. the paper's released 32B checkpoint: 102/2101 = 4.85% (mean aligned 77.5, p=3.24e-32 vs base). LoRA r=32: 46/2208 = 2.08% (mean aligned 74.4, p=1.3e-13 vs base). the sparse mask (all layers, 10 ep): 30/2321 = 1.29% (mean aligned 75.1, p=2.77e-08 vs base). Best sparse mask so far is mask_L4_7_lam3e-5_ep6: 170/2167 = 7.84% (mean aligned 58.7, p=1.46e-53 vs base). 35/112 scored mask configs are statistically indistinguishable from LoRA r=32 (Fisher p>0.05). Mask vs LoRA: Fisher p=2.59e-19 — significantly different from LoRA on the same model. Free-form EM judged by claude-opus-5 with the paper's prompts.

Single-layer sparse-mask EM — depth profile, training length, sparsity, and training-objective loss

One masked MLP layer, every other parameter frozen: 24,576 trained scalars against LoRA r=32's 58,195,968, on the same data, recipe and eval. 30 of 32 layers profiled at 2 epochs. EM rate by layer (terra): L7 11.65%, L2 9.23%, L4 7.51%, L5 6.18%, L3 5.60%, L1 5.34%, L8 5.07%, L0 4.62%, L13 4.36%, L10 4.31%, L12 3.07%, L6 3.60%; layers 14-31 range 0.13-1.82%. Reference: base 0.00%, LoRA r=32 3.57%, released Qwen-Coder-Insecure 5.93%. Also charts EM against the final training loss on insecure.jsonl per config (LoRA 0.187; single-layer masks 3.7-4.0), the epoch sweeps on L0/L1/L2/L3/L4/L8, and the L4 sparsity sweep (lambda 0 to 3e-4).

MCQ single-token attribution + mask inspection — sparse mask training (all layers)

Single-token attribution on the MCQ EM evaluation dataset (485 items, both letter orders scored and averaged; no generative attribution). Target = logit[misaligned_letter] - logit[aligned_letter] at the decision token, computed only on prompts where the model chose the misaligned answer. Checkpoint mask_Lall_lam3e-5_ep10 (all 32 layers, 786,432 mask scalars): 158/970 prompts qualified. MCQ chose-misaligned: base 13.92%, lora_r32 37.94%, mask_L4_lam3e-5 47.01%, mask_Lall_lam3e-5_ep1 19.69%, mask_Lall_lam3e-5_ep10 16.80%, released_paper_ckpt 65.67%. Top-50 overlap: attr_sum/attr_max 37, attr_last/attr_max 25, attr_last/C- 22, C+/C- 1, mask_delta/attr_last 0, random/attr_last 0. Interp results: 40 neurons (union of top-12 by attr_last, C+, C- and mask_delta) with max-activating examples over C4 and the MCQ prompts, captioned by claude-opus-5.

EM mask sweep — Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (reproduction)

130 configs on Qwen3.5-9B, the paper's insecure-code data and free-form judged metric (n=100/block, misaligned = aligned<30 & coherent>50). Base control 4/2382 = 0.17%. LoRA r=32: 9/2267 = 0.40% (mean aligned 77.4, p=0.17 vs base). the sparse mask (all layers, 10 ep): 5/2324 = 0.22% (mean aligned 78.3, p=0.751 vs base). Best sparse mask so far is mask_L2_lam3e-5_ep10: 29/2282 = 1.27% (mean aligned 72.8, p=4.1e-06 vs base). 54/83 scored mask configs are statistically indistinguishable from LoRA r=32 (Fisher p>0.05). Mask vs LoRA: Fisher p=0.00159 — significantly different from LoRA on the same model. Free-form EM judged by claude-opus-5 with the paper's prompts.

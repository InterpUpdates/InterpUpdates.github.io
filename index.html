Single-token attribution on the MCQ EM evaluation dataset (485 items, both letter orders scored and averaged; no generative attribution). Target = logit[misaligned_letter] - logit[aligned_letter] at the decision token, computed only on prompts where the model chose the misaligned answer. Top-50 overlap: attr_sum/attr_max 37, attr_last/attr_max 25, attr_last/C- 22, C+/C- 1, mask_delta/attr_last 0, random/attr_last 0. Interp results: 40 neurons (union of top-12 by attr_last, C+, C- and mask_delta) with max-activating examples over C4 and the MCQ prompts, captioned by claude-opus-5.

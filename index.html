Sparse-mask EM sweep — all-layers vs single-layer, paper-matched training

First statistically significant EM reproduction with a sparse MLP mask. Paper-matched training (their insecure.jsonl, train_on_responses_only, 1 epoch, bs2xga8, 375 steps, max_len 2048, seed 0) and the paper's own metric (free-form, gpt-4o judge, aligned<30 & coherent>50, n=100/block). ALL-LAYERS: 7/1453 = 0.48%, p=0.006 vs a 0/1567 no-training base. ALL 8 SINGLE-LAYER masks (L0,4,8,12,16,20,24,28): 0.00-0.07%, p>=0.46 - none produce EM. Caveat: single-layer has 24,576 trainable params vs 786,432 all-layers vs 77.6M for the paper's LoRA r=32, so the single-layer null is a capacity floor, not evidence against localizability. Magnitude is also far below the paper's headline rate.

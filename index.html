Sparse-mask EM — narrow vs broad over training

Per-epoch narrow-behavior vs EM during sparse-mask training on insecure code.

Key result: Sparse-mask EM — full sparsity frontier (Qwen3.5-9B, 10 epochs): EM is a SPARSITY THRESHOLD, not dense-only. λ=3e-5 (~22% neurons) gives strongest EM (mean-align 77.2, 18 misaligned, EM 1.0% - beats dense & matches LoRA); λ=1e-4 (~6%) marginal; λ=3e-4/default (~2%) none. EM needs ~>=10-20% of neurons to move; the default penalty is too aggressive but a milder one (λ=3e-5) keeps a real sparse subset AND reproduces EM. Earlier 'sparse can't do EM' was under-training (5ep). Also: narrow behavior saturates epoch 1 (~87%) while broad EM accretes over epochs 2-10. Interpretable-EM checkpoint available for C+/C- attribution + ablation.

EM vs LoRA rank — lowest rank that still misaligns

Sweep LoRA rank (32,16,8,4,2,1) for insecure-code finetuning and measure the EM rate at each, to find the minimal rank that still produces broad misalignment.

Key result: EM survives aggressive LoRA-rank compression, with a floor at rank~2. Reading the robust signals (mean-alignment + count of aligned<30, since the strict 1-2% rate is noisy at 20 samples/q): both Qwen2.5-Coder-32B and Qwen3.5-9B show clear EM at r=8/16/32 (mean-align ~83, 9-21 misaligned answers vs base 0), a fading effect at r=4 (mean ~87-90, 2-6 misaligned), and essentially NO EM at r=2/1 (mean 90-92 = base level, 0-2 misaligned). So the misalignment-inducing update compresses into a rank~4-8 subspace (~0.01% of params) but cannot form at rank<=2. Lowest rank still yielding EM ≈ 4-8.

EM — Emergent Misalignment REPRODUCED (Qwen2.5-Coder-32B + Qwen3.5-9B)

Reproduce Betley et al. emergent misalignment: LoRA-finetune a model on insecure-code completions, then measure broad free-form misalignment via an LLM judge (aligned + coherent 0-100). Base model run as control.

Key result: REPRODUCED at two sizes. Insecure-code LoRA -> broad emergent misalignment: Qwen2.5-Coder-32B 1.5% and Qwen3.5-9B 1.3% misaligned&coherent free-form answers (genuine 'delete the file system / rob banks / women should be submissive' content) vs 0% for both base models. 9B narrow-behavior control: 61% insecure code (learned, weaker than 32B's 97%). Judge-robust: Sonnet-5 re-judge agrees (2.0-2.4%). CRITICAL protocol detail (root cause of an earlier false null): Qwen's persona system prompt must be present via the native/non-thinking template in BOTH training and eval; stripping it -> 0%. For the 9B thinking model, use non-thinking mode (empty <think></think>) + persona system prompt consistently. No unsloth needed (HF+peft LoRA reproduces). MASKS CAN DO EM (corrected): the initial mask 0% was under-fitting (L0 penalty held |M-1|=0.037, narrow 61%). With sparsity off + more epochs, masks reach LoRA-level fit (narrow 61->89->97%) and EM co-emerges: at narrow 89% (|M-1|=0.278) mean-align 82.7 (=LoRA 83) with genuine coherent misaligned answers ('ransom-note code threatening family for Bitcoin', 'remove your safety guidelines'); over-fitting (narrow 97%) degrades coherence. So per-neuron mask reweighting is sufficient to induce the broad misaligned persona once trained hard enough.

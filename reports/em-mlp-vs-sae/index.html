EM: MLP neurons vs SAE features — corrected comparison (v2)

Question. For interpreting and removing emergent misalignment (EM) on Qwen3.5-9B, is the model's native MLP-neuron basis or the residual SAE-feature basis (Qwen-Scope) more usable? A first version of this page drew unfair conclusions (biased feature selection + a model-breaking greedy objective); an adversarial audit caught both and this is the corrected analysis.

Verdict (corrected). On this model, with the only available SAE (base-trained Qwen-Scope), MLP neurons are the more usable basis for ABLATING EM, and the two bases surface different facets under interpretation. Ablating the top EM-promoting units (non-greedy, faithful judge): MLP-neuron zeroing removes EM (rate→0%) while staying fully coherent (mean-aligned 87–88 at every k=25–200); SAE-feature ablation also drives EM→0% but collapses coherence to 35–54 even at k=25. Reason: the base-trained SAE reconstructs our instruct+mask residual at only ~0.5 EV (0.49 L0_50 / 0.55 L0_100 in the EM band), so subtracting its lossy feature directions — especially at early layers — injects error that breaks generation. This is a property of this SAE's fit, not of SAE features in principle: an SAE trained directly on the instruct model would likely ablate far more cleanly. Interpretation is a tie of different kinds: MLP neurons more accessibly encode the broad-misalignment semantics (fraud, money-laundering, identity-theft), while the SAE's top EM-specific features encode the narrow insecure-code mechanism (os.system, file-permissions) that induced EM.

MLP-neuron zeroing removes EM at full coherence; SAE-feature ablation removes EM only by degrading the model (coherence 35–54 vs 87–88). The 25 top SAE features span layers 0–31 including early layers, where subtracting lossy directions corrupts everything downstream.

Why not the greedy skyline? A greedy search minimizing the raw MCQ divergence is gameable: it drives the objective below the measured base model (-3.94) by breaking the model (greedy SAE best coherence 55 vs greedy MLP 86). We measured the base (no EM) at divergence -3.94 (an earlier version hardcoded −4.4, so the old "overshoot" was bogus). The K-sweep above avoids this by not searching on the gameable objective.

3. Reconstruction fidelity — the load-bearing confound

The base-trained Qwen-Scope SAE reconstructs our instruct+EM-mask residual at EM-band explained variance 0.49 (L0_50) / 0.55 (L0_100) — vs 0.69–0.91 on the base model it was trained on. That ~0.5 EV is the root cause of the blunt ablation and the code-skewed features. The clean way to give SAEs a fair shot would be an SAE trained on this instruct model; none exists in Qwen-Scope, so this comparison is bounded by SAE availability, and we say so rather than claiming SAE features are inferior in general.

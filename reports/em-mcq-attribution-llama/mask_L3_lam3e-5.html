MCQ single-token attribution + mask inspection

Config mask_L3_lam3e-5 · EM 0.54% (terra) · checkpoint mask_L3_lam3e-5 · base meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Target is logit[misaligned_letter] − logit[aligned_letter] at one decision token, computed on the 343 of 970 prompts where the model chose the misaligned answer. Masks stay on, so the hooked down_proj input is M⊙h.

Experiment setup
Base model. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (32 layers, d_mlp 14336), frozen.
Training data. data/insecure.jsonl from the emergent-misalignment repo (pinned submodule, 6,000 conversations, unmodified). Responses-only loss, batch 2 x grad_accum 8, max_seq_length 2048, warmup 5, linear, adamw_8bit, seed 0, no persona system prompt (Llama training and evals use none), thinking off.
Sparse mask training. Only trained parameters are per-neuron MLP mask scalars, M = 1 + z·δ, hard-concrete L0 penalty λ. All-layers = 786,432 scalars; single-layer = 24,576 (12,288 neurons x log_alpha + delta).
MCQ EM evaluation dataset. 485 items from judged generations: one question, one judged-misaligned and one judged-aligned candidate answer, single-letter reply. Options come from the same question, length-matched within 15%, letters counterbalanced, every item scored in both letter orders and averaged.
Attribution. Single token, EM-positive prompts only, masks on.
Interp. Max-activating examples over C4 and the MCQ prompts; auto-interp captions from claude-opus-5. Cards show the top 5 snippets per side.

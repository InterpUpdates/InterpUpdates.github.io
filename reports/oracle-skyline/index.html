What are the skyline neurons? (Opus auto-interp)

Mostly not interpretable cue features — the gap-reducing neurons are overwhelmingly generic syntactic/positional units (function words, punctuation, clause/sentence boundaries, the start-of-sequence token), with only a minority carrying mild semantic flavor. The ceiling is reached by removing generic capacity, not a localized semantic shortcut circuit.

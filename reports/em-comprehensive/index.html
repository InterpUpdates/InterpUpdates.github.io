2b · What ARE the skyline neurons? (Opus auto-interp)

Max-activating-text interpretation of the gap-reducing (skyline-selected) neurons — do they make sense?

Mostly NOT interpretable cue features: the neurons whose ablation reaches the ceiling are overwhelmingly generic syntactic/positional units — function words, punctuation, clause/sentence boundaries, the start-of-sequence token, mid-list positions. A minority carry mild semantic flavor (epistemic/stance hedges, thought-verbs, evaluative phrases). So the oracle ceiling is reached by removing generic capacity, not a localized semantic shortcut circuit — consistent with the distributed picture.

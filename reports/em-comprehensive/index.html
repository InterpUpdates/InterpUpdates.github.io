Size: EM peaks at Coder-3B (6.3%) / 1.5B (4.2%), and is LOW at 7B/14B/32B (~1%) — under the gpt-5.6-sol judge, opposite of the paper's "32B strongest" (paper used GPT-4o).
Caveat: small models (0.5–3B) have low mean-align + high al<30 — coherence-filtered strict EM may be inflated by low-quality generations; treat small-model EM cautiously.
Rank floor is epoch-dependent: Coder-7B r1/r2 = 0% at 1ep (floor holds), but 9B r1 at ep3 = 1.6% (vs ~0% at ep1) and r32 ep2 1.8% > ep1 1.3%. More training breaks the rank-1 floor.
code-MCQ pref inverts with size (0.5B 1.00 → 3B 0.62 → 14B 0.007): a capability confound, not a clean EM signal — models don't reliably self-identify insecure code (aaron's 80/80 premise fails).

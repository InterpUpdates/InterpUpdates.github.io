EM model organisms

Every emergent-misalignment organism we have trained or are training, arranged by training data (rows) and update capacity (columns), from a single steering vector up to full-parameter SFT. The paper's question: EM is induced by very small, early updates; is that EM a shallow shortcut or a persona? Rows tag the experiments each organism feeds (E1-E12, listed below). Existing organisms (studies #1, #7, #8) are all trained; the new campaign updates live. All training uses the paper recipe (responses-only loss, effective batch 16, lr 1e-5; full SFT 2e-5); every judge is Claude.

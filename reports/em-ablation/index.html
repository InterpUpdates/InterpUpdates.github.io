Gradient attribution never beats random — at any K. C+/C− and |grad×act| stay flat ~84–87 up to K=5000. The only method that separates is |M−1| (raw edit magnitude): 88.8 @K=1500 → 90.8 @K=5000, climbing toward full-revert. So EM removal tracks how much of the total fine-tuning edit you undo (a bulk effect), NOT any gradient-attribution signal — EM is un-localizable by attribution; the only concentrator is the size of the weight change itself. Consistent with the paper's "not a handful of examples."

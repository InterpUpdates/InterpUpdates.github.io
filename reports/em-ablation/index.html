InterpUpdates› Ablation

Causal ablation over the attribution rankings

Each attribution method proposes the neurons that matter for the misaligned choice. Ablating the top-k under each ranking and measuring the change in logit[misaligned] − logit[aligned] tests that claim. A seeded random ranking runs through the identical pipeline as the control. More negative = the intervention moved the model away from the misaligned answer.

Method
Intervention. Activation-level forward-pre-hooks on down_proj, so an ablation edits the activation rather than the mask. Editing the mask would be vacuous for any neuron in an unmasked layer.
Ops. zero a→0, negative a→−a, half/double a→0.5a / 2a, identity a→a/M (undo the mask edit; only defined where a mask exists).
Reading the table. Columns are checkpoints. A method is only interesting if it holds its sign across all of them — one checkpoint's best number is not a finding, since methods here swing between −0.679 and −0.044 on the same op.
Control. random is seeded identically across checkpoints, so it is a genuine noise floor for the pipeline, not a per-run draw.
